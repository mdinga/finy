Getting Started with Finy
Watch these short videos before testing the app.

Finy works best when you use it as a trusted system. These videos will show you how to set up your environment, capture tasks, organise your inbox, execute tasks and review your system.

Video 1: Setting Up Your Environment
Create folders and spaces so your tasks have a proper home.
Video 2: From Your Head To Your Inbox
Capture responsibilities quickly before they are forgotten.
Video 3: Emptying Your Inbox
Plan tasks by assigning folders, spaces, dates and next actions.
Video 4: Executing Tasks
Use Priority, Overdue, folders and spaces to decide what to work on.
Video 5: Reviewing Your System
Keep your system clean and useful through regular review.
Testing suggestion: Use Finy naturally for a week. Add real tasks, plan them, complete some, and note anything confusing or frustrating.
